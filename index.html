Sinaloa Cafe is Mondeor's home for premium cannabis — a space where quality, education, and community converge. Located at 144 Columbine Ave, we've become the destination of choice for discerning cannabis enthusiasts across Johannesburg's South.

Our expert team curates every product with care, ensuring that every visit leaves you better informed and better served. Whether you're a first-time visitor or a seasoned connoisseur, Sinaloa is your trusted guide.
